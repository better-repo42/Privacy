Privacy Policy
ClassTrack
Attendance made simple
Overview
ClassTrack is a privacy-first attendance management app designed for teachers. We believe your students' data belongs to you — and only you.
Data Collection
ClassTrack does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All information you enter — including student names, roll numbers, and attendance records — is stored exclusively on your device.
No data is sent to any server or cloud service
No account creation or login is required
No analytics or tracking of any kind
All data lives in your browser's local storage
Student Data
Any student information you enter (names, roll numbers, class, section, etc.) is stored only on your device and is never shared with third parties. You have full control to edit or delete this data at any time through the app's Reset function.
Advertisements
ClassTrack may display advertisements via Google AdMob to support ongoing development. Google AdMob may use cookies and similar technologies to serve relevant ads based on your browsing activity. This ad data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and is separate from the attendance data stored locally on your device.
Ad cookies are managed by Google, not ClassTrack
Your attendance data is never used for ad targeting
You can opt out of personalized ads via Google's Ad Settings
Children's Privacy
ClassTrack is a tool for teachers and is not directed at children. Student data entered by teachers is stored locally and is never transmitted or used for any purpose other than attendance tracking within the app.
